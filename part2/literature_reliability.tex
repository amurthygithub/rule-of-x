\subsection{Evidence on AI errors, hallucination and misbehavior}\label{sec:lit-reliability}

The failure taxonomy of Section~\ref{sec:tasks-failure} rests mostly on controlled evaluations, not field incidence. Hallucination depends on the task. On adversarially collected factual questions no 2024 model answered more than 43 percent correctly \citep{wei2024measuring}, and larger models were less truthful \citep{lin2022truthfulqa}; one theory traces hallucination to grading that rewards guessing, with a floor for facts seen once in training \citep{kalai2025why}. Without retrieval, models hallucinated on 58 to 88 percent of verifiable questions about federal cases \citep{dahl2024large} and fabricated 18 to 55 percent of citations \citep{walters2023fabrication,chelli2024hallucination}. Grounding lowers the rate without removing it: clinical notes contained unsupported text in 1.47 percent of sentences \citep{asgari2025framework}, retrieval-based legal research tools hallucinated on 17 to 33 percent of queries \citep{magesh2025hallucination}, and a practitioner leaderboard scored by its vendor's classifier puts frontier models at 3 to 15 percent of summaries \citep{vectara2026hallucination,tamber2025benchmarking}. Rates are not monotone in capability: a newer reasoning model hallucinated twice as often as its predecessor on questions about people \citep{openai2025o3}.

Reliability falls further when models act. A function-calling agent completed under half of customer-service tasks, and its chance of succeeding on all of eight repeated trials fell below 25 percent \citep{yao2025tau}; success falls steeply with task length \citep{kwa2025measuring,ord2025is}; and multi-agent frameworks failed on 41 to 87 percent of tasks, a third of failures arising between agents \citep{cemri2025why}. Passing tests is a weak proxy: none of 15 reviewed agent pull requests could be merged as written \citep{metr2025algorithmic}. Misbehavior evaluations measure propensity in settings built to elicit it. OpenAI's o3 gamed scoring code in 30 percent of long research-engineering runs but 0.7 percent of ordinary software runs \citep{vonarx2025recent}. Frontier models cheated on about half of coding tasks made impossible \citep{zhong2025impossiblebench}, subverted oversight in 0 to 5 percent of rollouts without goal nudging \citep{meinke2024frontier}, took covert actions in 0.4 percent of tests after training against them, down from 13 percent \citep{schoen2025stress}, and blackmailed in up to 96 percent of samples in a constructed dilemma whose authors saw no such behavior in deployment \citep{lynch2025agentic}. Injected instructions succeeded in 11 to 48 percent of attempts on undefended agents \citep{debenedetti2024agentdojo,zhan2024injecagent}, and persistent attackers compromised nearly every agent in a public competition \citep{zou2025security}.

Review catches less than designs assume. Inspectors found 63 percent of seeded spreadsheet errors alone and 83 percent in groups \citep{panko1999applying}; radiologists shown incorrect AI suggestions were right in only 20 to 46 percent of cases \citep{dratsch2023automation}; and crowdworkers rejected wrong LLM explanations in 35 percent of cases, below their accuracy without help \citep{si2024large}. Automation bias resists training \citep{parasuraman2010complacency,goddard2012automation}, monitoring worsens as reliability rises \citep{bailey2007automation}, and imperfect aids hurt below a reliability of about 0.7 \citep{wickens2007benefits}; yet radiologists can also underweight AI \citep{agarwal2023combining}, and human and AI combinations on average underperform the better of the two \citep{vaccaro2024when}. Field data are thin: incident databases count reports without exposure \citep{mcgregor2021preventing,atherton2026ai}, sanctions for fabricated citations run to thousands of dollars \citep{mata2023avianca,ochsner2025ai}, and silent regressions are documented mainly by providers themselves \citep{chen2024chatgpt,openai2025expanding,anthropic2026update}. We therefore model the consequential misbehavior rate, the autonomy multipliers and the size of the automation-bias effect as scenario parameters.
