# Modern theory map (spec Section 20): AI-era theory and evidence, 2018 to 2026, by model component

Prepared 29 October 2026 for the integration theorist. Keys refer to `part2/refs_modern.bib` (new), `part2/refs_part2.bib` and `refs.bib` (reused). Every item was verified at a publisher, DOI, NBER, arXiv, SSRN, RePEc/IDEAS or issuing-body page; the audit trail is `notes/ref_verification_modern.md`. Working papers (WP), preprints and conference drafts are labeled. Result labels refer to `part2/theory_core.tex` (prefix `tasks-`), `part2/theory_ext.tex` (prefix `ext-`) and `sections/model.tex` (Part I). `theory_regimes.tex` and `theory_product.tex` do not exist yet, so the ownership and product rows cite spec Sections 16 and 17.

How to read the third column: **supports** means the modern result is consistent with ours or gives an independent derivation; **sharpens** means it adds a parameter, sign condition or calibration we should adopt; **changes** means one of our assumptions or statements should be modified; **contradicts** means the evidence runs against a statement as currently worded.

| shahidi2025coasean (WP); hadfield2025economy (preprint); rothschild2025agentic (preprint) | AI agents that search, negotiate and transact lower transaction costs sharply, enable new market designs and create new frictions (congestion, price obfuscation); markets of agents need institutions. | **Sharpens** prop:tasks-window: the Coasean force is a fall in the coordination share κ_O (and possibly ζ_O). ## Ranked list: the ten changes that matter most

Each item gives the change, the evidence, and recommended wording or a modified formula for the integration theorist.

**1. Review is imperfect: add a detection rate and an automation-bias term.**
Evidence: dratsch2023automation, agarwal2023combining, vaccaro2024when, bucinca2021trust, nist2024artificial.
Modify ass:tasks-review: "Reviewing a unit costs w_j t^R_j and detects its error with probability d_j ∈ (0,1]." Then the quality cost becomes c^Q_j(ξ) = w_j t^R_j ξ + e_j L_j[1 − d_j ξ(1+ln(1/ξ))], the economic share is exp(−w_j t^R_j/(d_j e_j L_j)), the undetected error rate is ε_j = e_j(1 − d_j ξ), and the reliability share is (1 − ε̄_j/e_j)/d_j. New breakpoint for prop:tasks-review(d) and prop:tasks-breakpoints(3): the reliability target is infeasible at any review share if d_j < 1 − ε̄_j/e_j. In the running example (exception handling at readiness 0.9: e = 0.072, ε̄ = 0.05, L = $60, w t^R = $4.32), automation costs $3.79 at d = 1, ties augmentation ($4.10) at d = 0.9, costs $4.44 at d = 0.8 and $6.43 at d = 0.455 (Dratsch's very experienced readers), and is infeasible below d = 0.31. Suggested sentence: "Field and laboratory evidence shows that reviewers miss many AI errors, so the value of review depends on its detection rate as well as its cost; below a detection threshold no amount of review meets a reliability target." Carry d_j through prop:tasks-augment(c), cor:ext-floor (the floor now buys less reliability) and prop:ext-team(d) (Amdahl bound with oversight). Optional second parameter: a false-override rate on correct AI outputs (agarwal2023combining's under-weighting).

**2. Augmentation has a quality cost and is task-type dependent.**
Evidence: dellacqua2026navigating, vaccaro2024when, agarwal2023combining.
Modify ass:tasks-modes: c^0_j(U) = w_j t^U_j + e^U_j L_j, where e^U_j can exceed the in-house error rate for tasks outside the frontier. Add a decision/creation flag. Wording for prop:tasks-augment: "For decision tasks in which the AI is more accurate than the person, blended augmentation is typically dominated; the relevant comparison is automation with routing against in-house work. Augmentation is the natural mode for creation tasks and for tasks in which people outperform the AI." The single-crossing ladder of prop:tasks-modes(b) still holds because the quality term does not depend on π, but U may never be on the frontier.

**3. Process complementarity: cite the independent O-ring derivation, allow negative integration premia, and make chained errors position dependent.**
Evidence: gans2026oring, agrawal2024artificial, sinha2025illusion, dziri2023faith, bonney2026microstructure.
(a) In the intuition after prop:tasks-aon add: "Gans and Goldfarb (2026) reach the same conclusion, that adoption is discrete and bundled, from quality complementarity in a worker's time allocation; our condition adds handoffs, chained errors and released overhead on a process graph."
(b) In prop:tasks-complement(a), state explicitly that η^×_e ≥ 0 can fail when AI raises decision variance at an interdependent edge (Agrawal, Gans and Goldfarb 2024), and that supermodularity then needs a coordination mechanism whose cost belongs in the integration build F.
(c) Generalize lem:tasks-oring(a) to step-specific ε_k: the target requires Σ_k −ln(1−ε_k) ≤ −ln q̄_p, and if ε_k rises with position (self-conditioning), the common-ε bound overstates the feasible chain length.

**4. Capability path: carry two reliability forms and domain-specific horizons.**
Evidence: ord2025is, kwa2025measuring, kwa2025how, yao2025tau.
Modify ass:ext-capability(ii): "We consider two forms: logistic in log length, which fits METR's data and gives χ(q) = [q/(1−q)]^{η_r}, and a constant hazard, which gives χ(q) = 1/log₂(1/q)." Report prop:ext-feasible(b) as ranges: 1.64 to 2.32 doublings at 80%, 3.76 to 4.93 at 95% and 6.11 to 7.70 at 99% (0.93 to 1.32, 2.13 to 2.80 and 3.46 to 4.36 years at 207 days). Replace "applying it to other activities is an assumption" in (i) with domain-specific (H_0,g, T_d,g): GUI computer-use tasks start 40 to 100 times lower at similar doubling rates, and physical work (self-driving) improves at about 0.6 doublings a year, so (iv) should use a slower T_d for physical work rather than a pure lag. Estimate e_j from pass^k-type consistency for high-volume activities.

**5. Make or buy: state the two opposing AI forces on the firm boundary.**
Evidence: shahidi2025coasean, garicano2026vanishing, demirci2025who, hui2024short.
Add to prop:tasks-window(c): "The make-or-buy volume v^MB rises as AI lowers the coordination share κ_O (the Coasean force: agents make markets cheaper to use) and falls as AI lowers the in-house build cost F^A relative to the vendor's pooled cost (the knowledge-cost force: AI erodes specialists' scale economies). Which dominates is empirical." Add that the vendor price for the residual, harder tasks can rise as easy tasks are automated (Demirci et al.), so p_O should depend on task difficulty in the engine.

**6. Private versus social value, and expertise-adjusted residual wages.**
Evidence: acemoglu2026automation, autor2025expertise, autor2024new, babina2025firm, gupta2026generative.
Add a remark after cor:tasks-weights: "The values computed here are private. When wages exceed workers' outside options, part of the saving is a transfer of rents, which Acemoglu and Restrepo (2026) estimate offsets 60 to 90 percent of automation's productivity gains at the aggregate level." In def:ext-org(i) cost review and sign-off at an expertise wage w^R_j ≥ w_j and report the residual organization's seniority mix; flag the apprenticeship gap when junior production roles disappear.

**7. Calibrate benefit realization and the J-curve to the adoption evidence.**
Evidence: humlum2025still, bick2026rapid, bonney2026microstructure, bonney2024tracking, brynjolfsson2021productivity.
In ass:tasks-transition(iv) and the engine defaults, set the central benefit-realization share b̄_p well below one for augmentation-led programs, with a note: "Adoption has been rapid, but two years after ChatGPT effects on earnings and hours above 2 percent are ruled out in Danish administrative data, generative AI assisted 1 to 5 percent of US work hours, and two-thirds of adopting US firms use AI mainly to augment." Add to the intuition of prop:tasks-readiness: "Most firms appear to sit below the readiness threshold, where only augmentation pays, and firm performance rises with the breadth of AI integration, as the integration premium predicts."

**8. Rents, data and inference demand: make the scarce-input condition explicit.**
