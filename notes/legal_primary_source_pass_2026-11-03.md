### C. Statements checked and found Correct

L. 119–21, title VII, § 70302(a)," 139 Stat. 190; §70302(e)(1): "shall apply to amounts paid or incurred in taxable years beginning after December 31, 2024" (transition rules in (e) and (f), as the bib note says).
2. model.tex:375: 100% additional first-year allowance for qualified property acquired after 19 January 2025 under §168(k) as amended by §70301. 168(k)(1)(A): "an allowance equal to 100 percent of the adjusted basis"; §70301(c)(1) effective date as above; Notice 2026-11 §2.02(1): "a permanent 100 percent additional first year depreciation deduction for qualified property" acquired "after January 19, 2025". Computing equipment qualifies under (k)(2)(A)(i)(I) (recovery period of 20 years or less). Optional refinement: the allowance is taken in the year placed in service, and the 40% (60%) transitional election of (k)(10) is available for the first taxable year ending after 19 January 2025.

5. model.tex:375: 174A(c) election to amortize "over such period of not less than 60 months". Correct.
