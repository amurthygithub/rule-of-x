## Findings (by severity; all minor)
1. **Abstract, "54 to 62 from sampling" (sections/abstract.tex:2; paper1/abstract_plain.txt).** Dropping "alone" loses the condition that the interval reflects sampling error only (the full text says "anchor-sampling interval ..., which holds the program and the target fixed"); a business reader can now read "from sampling" as "across samples". Fix: restore "from sampling alone" and recover 6 characters elsewhere, e.g. "We show that the rule draws" → "The rule draws" (−13), keeping the 1,920 limit.
2. **Remark S11 lists upfront costs that do not reproduce its κ_all (sections/appendix_proofs.tex:89; Table S11 row).** The remark and Table S11 give servers 21.2 + facility 11.4 + network 4.9 = 37.5 bn, but κ_all = 5.444 uses 37.883 bn (land 0.17, utility works 0.16 added), and the printed 82% at 15% growth would be 81% from the listed figures. The remark does say "land included" but never mentions utility works. Fix: add "plus land and utility works, 0.3 billion" (as a macro) in the remark and Table S11, or state "land and utility works included" in the code comment's wording; the basis itself (cash, straight-line, land not replaced) is stated clearly and is the right choice.
3. **Rounded κ does not reproduce the printed bars (sections/horizons.tex:46; Remark S11).** "3.0 times market growth ... 46% at 15%": 3.0 × 15 = 45; κ = 3.045. Same for 5.4 × 15 = 81 vs printed 82. Fix: print OwnKapSrv/OwnKapAll to two decimals (3.05, 5.44) or write "about 3".
4. **Abstract free-entry sentence no longer mentions automation (abstract.tex:2).** "With free entry into a homogeneous market, only a company with an input others cannot copy keeps more than a normal return, up to a ceiling set by AI's full costs" is true, but the point of F01 (competition passes automation's savings to customers) is gone, and "normal return" is less plain than the log's "lasting gain". If characters allow after finding 1, prefer the log's wording.
5. **Abstract F15 wording still ambiguous (abstract.tex:2).** "its margin with no growth spending, plus its market's growth net of the capital that growth needs": if "growth spending" is read as σ only, the margin at g = G is m0 − kG and kG is subtracted twice. The log's alternative ("the cash margin it earns growing with its market, plus that market's growth, plus ...") is unambiguous; or say "its margin at zero growth".
6. **Decision row overstates adoption (sections/appendix_protocol.tex:53).** "Adopted every fix ... classed as valid or partly valid" — M17 (partly valid, optional) was skipped and the optional M12 paper sentence was not added; the row says "three choices of her own" while the task/record speak of four decisions. Fix: "every non-optional fix ..., declining the optional M17 wording and the M12 sentence". The departures from the logs' wording (abstract F01, NB3 "below 4.6%", F11 "0.1%") are properly recorded in the change record.
7. **Layout: supplement p. 58 is two-thirds blank.** Remark S11 now runs past the [p] float Table S17 (p. 57) by 22 lines, and S10 starts with \clearpage on p. 59; this is the extra supplement page. Fix: move `\begin{table}[p]` of tab:fullcost (appendix_proofs.tex:40) after the remark, or use [!htp].
8. **F14 attribution (horizons.tex:11; supp_model.tex:35; Table S11).** "METR's run data suggest failed runs cost more" is the verifier's own computation from the public runs.jsonl (medians by outcome for three models), not a METR statement; S2.8 should say "our reading of METR's run data" as it does for the 0.4–1.1% figure.

The GAAP row's "from 0.0" is a 5th percentile of −0.03 (β_g < 0 in some samples with β_m > 0). Add half a sentence to the note.
