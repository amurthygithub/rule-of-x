## Overall

The reviewer's execution record is sound: every author command passed on the pinned commit and matched the committed outputs, so the computational objections of the first round are closed. Of the twenty new items, eighteen are valid and two partly valid; none is a reviewer error. But none of them moves a headline number: the 32.5% threshold, Table 4, the 58/53 path, the 7.5-year payback, the stress band and every empirical table stand. The three "majors" are, on inspection, (F01, F02) sentence-level overstatements in the abstract, executive summary and conclusion of results the paper already states correctly in Corollary 3(iv), the deferred-revenue bullet and Corollary 4, fixable in an hour with no rerun; and (F03) a side remark (Remark S11, one bullet on p. 60) whose two markup hurdles rest on a source share that is an annualized capital charge, not replacement cash. F03 needs the author's decision (rebuild on a stated cash basis, where the servers-only case lands near the printed values and the whole-data-center case about doubles them, or drop the numbers and keep the algebra); it affects nothing downstream. The genuinely new computational points are F08 (the $+\infty$ convention in the valuation bootstrap, which raises four printed upper ends and produces NaN that is printed as $\infty$) and F09 (a prose sentence contradicting its own table), both minor.

My judgment is therefore that GPT's three majors are not release-blocking: they call for a targeted pass over about fifteen sentences, one remark and two small code fixes, not a major revision.
