# Authoritative sources checked by specialist D

Retrieved 2026-11-02/03 in the local review session. These are source locators and short paraphrases, not legal advice.

1. PCAOB, **AS 2201**, current version: https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2201 . Consulted paragraphs .02, .22-.23, .62-.67, .A5, .A7-.A10. The page distinguishes a forthcoming version effective December 15, 2026; the review used the currently effective version. Relevant points: effectiveness cannot coexist with a material weakness; assess deficiencies together as well as separately; evaluate potential magnitude and likelihood; the assessment need not quantify likelihood as a percentage.
2. eCFR, **17 CFR 229.308**, https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-229/subpart-229.300/section-229.308 . Page displayed title 17 current through October 29, 2026. Consulted (a)(2)-(3) and evidence requirement. Management must name its evaluation framework and cannot conclude ICFR effective if a material weakness exists.
3. SEC, **Release 33-8810**, June 27, 2007, https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/interp/2007/33-8810fr.pdf . Consulted section II.B.1, Federal Register p.35332 (PDF p.10), including notes 47-48, and introduction. The guidance requires evaluating combinations and qualitative/quantitative factors; a numeric probability is not required.

Search also surfaced the PCAOB amended version effective December 15, 2026. It was not treated as already effective on the review date.
