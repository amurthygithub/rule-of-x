- Figure PDFs: inspected only as embedded in the PDF pages.

## Identification assessment (estimands)
- Psi_chi is a partial frontier conditioning on g >= g0 (a dominance cone), correctly distinguished from the local conditional quantile (g near g0) and from any firm's own frontier; the paper's Prop 4(iv) non-identification proof is elementary and correct (I re-derived: any slope/curvature can be generated by suitable (alpha', X', l', m0')), so the cross-sectional slope identifies nothing about the technology — the paper says exactly this and does not overclaim.
- alpha and X are not identified from the data (only alpha(X-k) bounded); paper treats alpha in [0.25,0.45] as scenario range; verified the spend-ratio logic sigma*/Delta* = alpha(X-k) symbolically.
- The decline of the top (2016-19 -> 2023-25) is descriptive; the reweighting decomposition is not robust (paper says so); competing readings listed. No causal claim is made. Consistent with what the data can support.
- Selection into the top tail (anchor = top 5% of cash score) inherits regression to the mean — disclosed in appendix_data.
- The 2025 "recovery" is partly acquisition-affected firm-years — disclosed with numbers (RecoveryEone vs RecoveryNoAcq).
