## 1. Strongest accurate version of the thesis
The Rule of 40 (growth + margin >= 40) and its growth-weighted variants (Rule of X) are
*price lines* (iso-score lines), not frontiers, on a concave efficient frontier of attainable
(growth, FCF-margin) pairs generated by spending on growth with diminishing returns in
remaining market headroom. Consequences:
1. The maximal score has a closed form: intercept + market growth net of capital + a
   growth surplus that is strictly transient (finite share-headroom budget, Prop 1).
2. A value-maximizing firm acts on a Rule-of-X score whose weight is the marginal value of
   revenue (marginal q of revenue) — CONDITIONAL on an unproved verification hypothesis (H).
   Investor screening weights (2-3) are durations, a different object; neither is identified
   by the cross-section of chosen points (Marschak-Andrews-type non-identification, Prop 3).
3. Full automation does not remove cost floors: compute, fees, cash taxes, an accountability
   floor (ICFR as a pass-fail control standard with common-mode failure algebra, Prop 5) and
   AI running costs bound the saturated cash score; at the central scenario a mature
   asset-light firm stays below a cash Rule of 100 unless market growth >~32.5%/yr
   (28-78% over the credible compute-share range; pass-through raises it). A Rule of 1000 is
   only a transient state needing ~9-20x today's demand pull.
4. Execution risk (failed transitions, limited change capacity, disruption) slows the
   approach to the frontier like a pace cut (Prop 6-7); central scenario: 41% of program
   benefits realized by 2036, score 59 [54,62], payback 7.5 yrs.
5. Competition: under free entry with replicable products, automation savings pass to
   customers except rents on non-replicable inputs, capped by the full-cost ceiling (Prop 4).
6. Empirics (292 software firms 2010-25, first-reported XBRL, partial-frontier estimators):
   cross-sectional 0.90-quantile frontier slope ~ -0.22 GAAP / -0.16 cash (far from -1;
   sign not familywise-robust), p95 of score fell 11.9/12.7 points 2016-19 -> 2023-25
   (GAAP survives Holm at 10% under most procedures; cash does not), spend ratio robust
   (3.0-3.5) but identifies only alpha(X-k) in [0.05,10.1] across methods; bought growth
   distorts scores (Bending Spoons case: 115.9 GAAP 2025 -> 87-114 charged as capital).
