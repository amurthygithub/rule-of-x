# Round 3: units of the compute-share floor and one range for c_c at 2025 prices (3 Nov 2026)

Coding agent, branch `review-round-3` (HEAD be86d24, not committed). Approved by the author; error verified by the lead.

## The error

The compute share at full automation is c_c = s_L x kappa: s_L the labor cost automated as a share of revenue (0.60
central, 0.50-0.65), kappa the compute cost per dollar of labor replaced (`data/tasks/params.yaml`
`c_c_compute_share.source`; `code/calibrate_horizons.py` `cc_path('prices2025')` = s_L x kappa_2025). The central case
converts correctly (0.60 x 0.10 = 0.06). The floor did not: METR's agent-run costs are kappa (about 1% of the human cost
per successful run, about 2% per completed task at the 50% success rate of the time-horizon definition), but the round-3
block `compute_credible` used 0.02 directly as c_c, a share of revenue. Correct: 0.02 x 0.60 = 0.012 of revenue (0.006 at
the cost of successful runs).

Second problem: three ranges for the same quantity (c_c at 2025 prices): the text's 2-60% (CcLo-CcHi), the stress test's
path drivers kappa 0.01-0.35 x s_L 0.50-0.65 (0.5-23%), and `CC['prices2025']` bounds (0.01, 0.20) mirrored by params.yaml
`c_c_compute_share.range`.

## The fix

One range everywhere: from the floor, kappa per completed task x s_L = 1.2% of revenue, to the value test, kappa = 1,
c_c = s_L = 60% at the central s_L.

## What the smoke runs show (n = 200, not for the paper)

Commands: `--n 200 --workers 2 --sobol-n 64 --search-budget 80 --corners 64 --amin-n 100` (seed 20261102, numpy Sobol),
and the same with `--seed 20261104 --compare-to <first>` under SALib 1.6.0. Both: 87 of 87 reproduction checks, shadow

assertion (< 0.05) are close to the line at n = 10,000. Shares on the 3,000 draws: G* <= 30% in 21% of draws.

Expected changes of verdicts after the full rerun (from the smoke and 3,000-draw runs; confirm on the full run):
