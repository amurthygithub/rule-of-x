## 2. Influence ranking and priority grounding

Engine sensitivity results do not exist yet (engine/QA, examples and tests are empty, and the package does not import: `synthetic.py` imports a missing `config.load_defaults`). The ranking is therefore reasoned from the formulas. Sourced or calibrated parameters with large effects (T_d, H0, the logistic slope, wages and tau_L) are not ranked because they need no grounding.

Headline results and their formulas:
- **39% ceiling growth threshold**: G* = 1 - (1 - tau)(1 - c_c - f - a_min) at k = 0, X = 1. dG*/dc_c = dG*/df = dG*/da_min = 1 - tau = 0.82; dG*/dtau = 1 - c_c - f - a_min = 0.745. One at a time over the paper's brackets: c_c 34.8-43.0%, a_min 37.7-41.4%, tau 36.7-41.1%, f 38.5-39.3%.
- **Horizon ranges** (tab:calib): R* = m0 + (1 - k)G + Pi, Pi = Pi_2025 mu^(1/(1-alpha)) (1-k)^(1/(1-alpha)). m0 spans about 20 points, G 10-25 points, and mu up to 2 with alpha 0.45 multiplies Pi by 3.5 (up to 54 points in 2028).
- **Automation share and value of the synthetic firm**: value = sum over activities of (c_min(H,O) - c_A) v, with c_A = review (xi x rho_rev x w t x premium) + quality cost (e, L) + inference (pi_h0 x class multiplier) + amortized build (F_A, T_A) + maintenance (mu_m F_A); feasibility needs H(t) x transfer x kappa_ctx >= d x chi(r_A); value is risk-adjusted by the success logit.
- **People plan**: residual organization = human work / span; transformation team = l_DI I/(wD) + l_AI F/(wD) + e_mnt N_A + e_PO n + A_aff/(D N_CM) + e_RG n_reg + e_SME n_build; pace capped by kappa_abs.
- **Regime IRRs** (prop:reg-metrics example: 11.6% gross with the program, 4.9% without): MOIC rises by [M_exit h_x dEB + debt-swept cash]/Eq0; driven by M_exit (practitioner), h_x and T_sea (theory presets, assumed), debt rate, leverage and the timing of run-rate (T_par, dip, success).
