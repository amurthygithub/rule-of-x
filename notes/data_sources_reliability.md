## Notes for the authors: what the evidence supports, and claims it does not

1. **Changes to engine defaults (applied automatically through params_rel).** h_retrieval 0.01 to 0.05 [0.015, 0.2]: 0.01 is a per-sentence rate, but the engine's unit is an output. h_open 0.05 to 0.15 [0.05, 0.5]. d_e 0.9 to 0.65 [0.45, 0.85]: the best precedent (Panko) is 0.63 alone and 0.83 in groups. d_h 0.6 to 0.4 [0.2, 0.6]: measured 0.35 (Si et al.) and 0.20-0.46 (Dratsch et al.). m0 1e-4 to 1e-3 [5e-5, 1e-2]: the sourced any-misbehavior rate is about 1e-2 per autonomous task; the consequential share is scenario. alpha_tail 1.8 to 1.5 [1.15, 2.3] from bank operational losses. b_ab 0.3 and nu_cm 2.0 unchanged (b_ab range now starts at 0 to allow automation neglect). The theory examples in theory_core.tex (catch rates 0.9-0.95 for ordinary errors, 0.5 for hallucinations) are optimistic against this evidence; 0.65 and 0.4 would be grounded.
2. **"Retrieval makes outputs hallucination-free."** Not supported. Commercial legal RAG tools hallucinated on 17-33% of preregistered queries (Magesh et al. 2025).
3. **"Hallucination rates are now about 1-3%."** True only for short-document summarization by the best models on a vendor's own classifier (Vectara best 1.8%; frontier 3-15%). Fact recall without retrieval runs 16-48% on PersonQA, citations 18-55% fabricated, and 5-22% of production responses with browsing had a major factual error (OpenAI's own data).
4. **"Newer, more capable models hallucinate less."** Not monotone: o3 and o4-mini hallucinated more than o1 on PersonQA (33% and 48% vs 16%); TruthfulQA found larger models less truthful.
5. **"A human in the loop catches the errors."** Not supported as a default. Reviewers rejected confidently wrong AI output in 20-46% of cases (radiology) and 35% (fact-checking), below their accuracy without AI; on average human-AI pairs do worse than the better of the two alone (g = −0.23). Fabricated citations passed review in a court filing and a A$440,000 consulting report.
6. **"Frontier models blackmail 96% of the time" or "models scheme".** These are rates in deliberately constructed dilemmas or elicitation environments. Without goal nudging, covert subversion was 0-5%; Anthropic reports no evidence of agentic misalignment in real deployments. The only production-traffic misbehavior rate found is OpenAI's deception rate, 2.1-4.8% of responses, self-reported.
7. **"Passing the benchmark means production-ready."** Not supported for code: 38% of agent PRs passed tests, 0 of 15 reviewed were mergeable (METR, small sample).
8. **Aggregate cost of AI errors.** No credible measured aggregate exists. The EY figure ($4.4M average, 99% with losses) is a self-reported survey of large firms; incident databases have no exposure denominator. Field losses found span C$812 to $200,000 per event (plus $5.4bn for one non-AI common-mode update), consistent with a heavy tail but not a distribution.
9. **Scenario-only parameters.** Consequential misbehavior rate per task (only an upper bound is sourced), autonomy multipliers, horizon elasticity, automation-bias magnitude, human post-hoc and approval catch rates for misbehavior, the hallucination floor share and grounding share, cascade, injection exposure frequency, cross-provider correlation, regression-burst value, and the control-effect parameters (least privilege, monitoring lag, kill-switch cap). Report each only as a scenario dimension.
