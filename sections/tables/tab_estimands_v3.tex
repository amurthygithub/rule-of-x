\par\vspace{2pt}\footnotesize\raggedright Notes: Audited core sample (data/panel\_v3.csv: SIC 7372, fiscal years 2010--2025, firm-years first reported in their own annual report, firms whose principal business is not software (rule R2) and the captive related-party filer excluded, acquisition flag of rule R4). E1 uses every firm-year and reported growth (baseline). E2 keeps firm-years without material acquisitions and re-admits acquisition-affected ones with pro forma organic growth where the original filings identify it. The imputation columns add the acquisition-affected firm-years E2 drops, with organic growth imputed by removing acquired revenue equal to the purchase price over a price-to-revenue multiple $M$, half in the deal year and half in the next. $\Psi_{0.90}$ slope (primary statistic): least-squares slope over $g_0=0,0.05,\dots,0.6$ of $\phi_{0.90}(g_0)$, the 90th percentile of the margin among firm-years with $g\geq g_0$ (dominance cone; each grid point at least 30 firm-years in the sample and 10 in a bootstrap draw); within periods the cells at 60\% growth are below 30 firm-years, so period slopes use $0\leq g\leq0.4$. Local $Q_{0.90}$ slope (descriptive): $\tau=0.90$ linear quantile regression of margin on growth for $0\leq g\leq0.6$, solved exactly. Size gradient: $\tau=0.95$ quantile regression of $R$ on log prior-year revenue, times $\ln 10$. Capital-intensity gap: P95 of $R$ above minus below the period median of capital expenditure and capitalized software over revenue, split re-estimated in each bootstrap draw; firm-years without the measure are not classified. $T(g_0)=g_0+\phi_{0.95}(g_0)$. Anchor: 2023--25 firm-years at or above the P95 of the cash score (calibration rules). Parentheses: firm-cluster bootstrap standard errors; brackets: 90\% percentile intervals; 999 replications, firms drawn from E1, every column on the same draws; E1 $-$ E2 paired. $^\dagger$: no interval (more than 5\% of draws undefined, or a frontier-point cell below 30 firm-years). Last column (D-3): the claim's sign / its 90\% percentile interval hold in E1, E2 and both imputation columns; for frontier points at 0 and 20\% and the anchor's $\Pi_{ub}$ no directional claim is made (the interval includes zero; no equivalence margin is tested).
